Soups & Stews: a dish tree drawn from culinary knowledge

Drafted 2026-11-20 for the curator. A reading draft, not an approved taxonomy.

What this document is

This is the list of soups and stews a knowledgeable cook would expect to find, across cuisines, written from culinary knowledge. It was not built from our recipe data. After the list was written, our data was laid against it, so that every dish on the list shows whether we already have it.

It answers the complaint that the earlier file only contained the dishes we already had. That earlier file was an export of our own data and nothing else.

How to read a row. Each row is one dish: the set of recipes a cook would accept as the same thing. A change of method or richness (roasted, creamy, slow cooker) is a variant of that dish, not a new one.

IN CATALOGWe already have this dish in the catalog. The last column gives the catalog's name for it and how many of our recipes are filed there.
RECIPES, NO DISHWe have no such dish, but recipes we hold call themselves this. Today they are filed under some other dish. The last column says how many.
NEWComes from culinary knowledge only. Nothing in our data mentions it. These are the gaps in what we have collected.

Columns. Dish is the proposed name, with other names it goes by in small type. Origin is the cuisine. What it is is one line that separates it from its neighbours. In our data is what we hold today.
