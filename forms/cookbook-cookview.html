Instructions
1
Heat the oven
Equipmentthe oven

Heat the oven to 375°F, rack centered — start it first so it’s up to temperature by the time the lasagna is built. (Bundles done first, of course.)

2
Press the ricotta
EquipmentA fine strainer + cheesecloth (3 qt+) · a bowl & a weight

Line the strainer with cheesecloth over the bowl, add the 2 lb ricotta, fold the cloth over, and set a weight on top. Leave it to drain at least 30 minutes (longer is better) — pressing out the whey keeps the filling from going watery. While it drains, do everything below.

3
Boil the noodles, undercooked
EquipmentA large pot (6–8 qt) · a colander

Boil the 16 lasagna noodles in well-salted water to 7–8 minutes — deliberately underdone, since they finish in the oven. Drain, rinse cool, keep moist; stack in 4 sets of 4.

4
Make the meat sauce
EquipmentA large deep skillet (12 in) · a wooden spoon

Heat the 2 Tbsp olive oil over medium-high. Add the Aromatics (1 cup onion + 1 Tbsp garlic) and cook 2–3 minutes. Add the Meats (1 lb beef + 1 lb sausage); break up and brown until no pink, then drain all but a few tablespoons of fat. Stir in the Tomato and the Sauce seasoning. Reduce to medium and simmer 10 minutes; set aside.

5
Mix the cheese filling
EquipmentA medium mixing bowl (3–4 qt) · a box grater

In the bowl, mix the Cheese filling — the drained ricotta, 1 cup shredded mozzarella, 2 beaten eggs, 1 cup grated Parmesan, ½ cup chopped parsley, 1 tsp salt, and ¼ tsp pepper — until combined.

6
Portion for assembly
EquipmentSeveral portioning bowls

Divide the made components so the layering needs no thought: split the Cheese filling into 3 and the meat sauce into 3; split the 8 oz fresh mozzarella (12 slices) into two piles of 6; split the 3 cups shredded mozzarella into two 1½-cup piles; and set out the tomato sauce as a 1 cup and a 1½ cup pour, plus the ½ cup Parmesan for the top.

7
Layer it
EquipmentA 9×13×3 in baking dish (9×13×3 in)

Build four layers from the portions. Bottom: the 1-cup sauce, 4 noodles, a cheese third, a meat third, one mozzarella pile (6 slices). Second: 4 noodles, a cheese third, a meat third, the other mozzarella pile. Third: 4 noodles, the last cheese third, the last meat third, one 1½-cup shredded pile. Top: the last 4 noodles, the 1½-cup sauce, the other 1½-cup shredded pile, the ½ cup Parmesan.

8
Cover & bake
EquipmentParchment + foil · a half-sheet pan + rack (13×18 in) · the oven

Lay parchment over the top, then seal tightly with foil. Set the dish on a rack in a foil-lined sheet pan. Bake at 375°F for 1 hour covered, then uncover and bake 30 minutes more, until bubbling and browned.

9
Rest, then cut
EquipmentA timer

Rest 15–30 minutes before cutting — it lets the layers set so they lift in clean squares. Cut 3×4 into 12 and serve.

A cook’s note

The tomato sauce is its own short recipe (or a good jarred marinara). Two pounds of any meat blend works. And don’t rush the rest — cutting too soon turns four careful layers into one delicious puddle.
