Small Plates & Pies

Spanakopita

Serves 8 · 1 hr 10 min · vegetarian

Crackling phyllo over a green, herb-flecked spinach and feta filling — the pie that turns up at every celebration.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb spinach, wilted & squeezed
  • 8 oz feta, crumbled
  • 1 bunch scallions, sliced
  • ½ cup dill, chopped
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • ½ lb phyllo
  • ½ cup olive oil, for brushing

Method

  1. Heat oven to 375°F. Combine spinach, feta, scallions, dill and eggs.
  2. Brush a pan with oil; layer half the phyllo, brushing each sheet.
  3. Spread the filling; top with remaining phyllo, brushing as you go.
  4. Score the top into squares. Bake 40–45 min until deep golden.

Coquilles Saint-Jacques

Serves 6 · 50 min · Julia Child

Scallops gratinéed with wine, garlic and herbs, finished under the broiler in their shells — a luscious French classic.

Ingredients

  • ⅓ cup minced yellow onions
  • 1 Tbsp butter
  • 1½ Tbsp minced shallot
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • 1½ lbs scallops, sliced
  • Salt & pepper
  • 1 cup sifted flour, in a dish
  • 2 Tbsp butter
  • 1 Tbsp olive oil
  • ⅔ cup dry white wine
  • ½ bay leaf
  • ⅛ tsp thyme
  • 6 buttered scallop shells
  • ¼ cup grated Swiss cheese
  • 2 Tbsp butter, in pieces

Method

  1. Cook the onions slowly in butter 5 min until tender. Stir in shallots and garlic; cook 1 min. Set aside.
  2. Dry the scallops; sprinkle with salt and pepper, roll in flour, shake off excess.
  3. Sauté the scallops quickly in very hot butter and oil for 2 minutes to brown lightly.
  4. Pour in the wine; add herbs and the onion mixture. Cover and simmer 5 min, then boil down the sauce until lightly thickened. Discard the bay leaf.
  5. Spoon into shells, sprinkle with cheese, dot with butter, and broil until bubbling and brown.
