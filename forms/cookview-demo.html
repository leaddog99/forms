Coquilles
Saint-Jacques

Serves 650 minJulia ChildFrench · gratin
scroll to cook

Scallops gratinéed with wine, garlic and herbs, finished under the broiler in their own shells — luscious, golden, and far easier than it looks.

Let's cook
Step 1

Sweat the aromatics

Cook the onions slowly in butter for about 5 minutes, until tender and translucent but not browned. Stir in the shallots and garlic and cook one minute more. Set aside.

you'll need · right here
  • 1 Tbsp butter
  • ⅓ cup minced onions
  • 1½ Tbsp minced shallot
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
🍳 small saucepan
Step 2

Dredge the scallops

Pat the scallops dry. Just before cooking, sprinkle with salt and pepper, roll in flour, and shake off the excess.

you'll need · right here
  • 1½ lbs scallops, sliced
  • 1 cup flour, in a dish
  • salt & pepper
🥣 shallow dish
Step 3

Sear — don't crowd

Sauté the scallops quickly in very hot butter and oil for about 2 minutes, just to brown them lightly. Work in batches so the pan stays hot.

you'll need · right here
  • 2 Tbsp butter
  • 1 Tbsp olive oil
🍳 10-inch enamel skillet, very hot
Step 4

Build the sauce

Pour the wine into the skillet with the scallops. Add the herbs and the cooked onion mixture. Cover and simmer 5 minutes, then uncover and boil the sauce down for a minute until lightly thickened. Correct the seasoning and discard the bay leaf.

you'll need · right here
  • ⅔ cup dry white wine
  • ½ bay leaf
  • ⅛ tsp thyme
  • the onion mixture
Step 5

Gratinée & serve

Spoon the scallops and sauce into the shells. Sprinkle with cheese and dot with butter. Broil 2–3 minutes until bubbling and golden brown. Serve at once.

you'll need · right here
  • 6 buttered scallop shells
  • ¼ cup grated Swiss cheese
  • 2 Tbsp butter, in pieces
🔥 broiler
✓

That's dinner.

Golden, bubbling, and in the shells — bon appétit.
