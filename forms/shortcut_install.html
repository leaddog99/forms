Save a recipe from Instagram

Or TikTok, Facebook, YouTube — any app with a Share button. Nothing to install.

Open the paste page

Keep it on your Home Screen: with that page open in Safari, tap Share ⬆️ → Add to Home Screen.

Using it

  1. On the post, tap Share (the paper plane on Instagram) → Copy Link.
  2. Open the paste page (the Home Screen icon) and tap Paste the link. Allow the paste when iOS asks.
  3. The recipe is read from the post and opens in the editor. Sign in if asked.

A private post, or one Instagram will not show without signing in, comes back with a note saying so. Open it in Safari and use the Grab Recipe bookmark, or share a screenshot of the recipe.

Optional: put “Save Recipe” in the share sheet itself

Skips the copy-and-paste. It is one Shortcut with one action.

Get the shortcut

Opens in Shortcuts. Tap Add Shortcut and you are done.

  1. Shortcuts app → + to make a new shortcut.
  2. Add the action Open URLs (search for it). Tap its URL field, paste the copied address, and with the cursor still at the end tap Shortcut Input in the strip of variables above the keyboard. The field should read …?share=1#u=Shortcut Input.
  3. Show it in the share sheet. Open the shortcut's details (the ⓘ button, or the menu under its name) and turn on Show in Share Sheet.
  4. Name it Save Recipe and tap Done.

Then, on a post: Share → Save Recipe. The first time, iOS asks whether the shortcut may open this site; choose Always Allow.

Give it to the family

Once yours works: in Shortcuts, tap-and-hold Save Recipe → Share → Copy iCloud Link, and paste that link into System settings under iOS share-sheet Shortcut. This page then shows a one-tap Get the shortcut for everyone else.

Prefer a bookmark in Safari? Install the Grab Recipe bookmarklet.
