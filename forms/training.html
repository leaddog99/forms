Labeling · is-recipe

Each row is a page the harvest/batch is_recipe filter judged, captured for training. The pill is the heuristic's call (KEPT = it thinks recipe · DROPPED = not) with its score. Click ✓ Recipe / ✗ Not a recipe / ⚠ Poor quality to record the correct answer — you only need to bother where the heuristic looks wrong (those rows are flagged). Corrections are the gold signal that lets a trained classifier beat the heuristic.
Rubric: mark ✓ Recipe only if a clean recipe can be reliably extracted — a distinct ingredient list and ordered steps. Use ⚠ Poor quality when a recipe is technically there but too messy to extract cleanly (ingredients bleed into prose, image-heavy) — it's a poor source, not a good keep. Reserve ✗ Not a recipe for pages with no recipe at all (roundups, articles, product pages). Tie-break: when genuinely torn between Recipe and Poor quality, pick ⚠ Poor quality — only clean, confidently-extractable pages earn ✓ Recipe. Rows are sorted borderline-first by default — nearest the score threshold, where the heuristic is least certain and your correction is worth the most (the score-100 JSON-LD certainties sink to the bottom).
