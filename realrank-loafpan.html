Everyday Sandwich Loaf

Step 5 of 8 · Equipment

Shape the dough into a tight log and settle it seam-side down into a greased 1 lb loaf pan. Cover and let it rise until it crests about an inch above the rim, 45–60 minutes.

RRealRank · the pan for this

A heavy metal pan is what gives this loaf its even, deep-brown crust and squared-off corners — and the Williams Sonoma Goldtouch Pro (1 lb) does it beautifully, with aluminized steel that won't warp and a coating that releases the loaf cleanly. The honest catch: America's Test Kitchen found it bakes almost identically to the plain USA Pan 1 lb it's based on, which costs roughly half as much. Buy the Goldtouch for the finish and the feel; buy the USA Pan if you only care how the bread turns out.
