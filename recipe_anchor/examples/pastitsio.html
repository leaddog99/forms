TraditionalPastitsio

👥 Serves 8⏱ ~2½ hr (1 hr active)🇬🇷 Greek pasta bake

Pastitsio lives or dies on whether the layers hold when you cut it — and this is the Akis approach that makes them behave: egg white through the pasta to set the base, yolks and cheese in the béchamel to enrich the top, a ladle of that béchamel stirred into the meat to bind the middle. The trick to a calm kitchen is the bundling below: everything is measured and combined first, so each step just reaches for what’s already there.

Bundled because each group goes into the pot at the same moment. Kept separate where timing or texture needs it — the tomato paste blooms on its own, and the roux comes together in the pan.

Method
1
Brown the beef
EquipmentA large deep pot / Dutch oven (5–6 qt) · a wooden spoon

Heat the ¼ cup olive oil in the pot over high heat. Add the 1½ lb ground beef in a single layer and let it sear undisturbed for 2–3 minutes before you break it up, then brown it well. Colour is flavour — beef that steams instead of sears makes a pale, flat sauce.

2
Build & simmer the meat sauce
EquipmentThe same pot (from step 1)

Stir in the Aromatics (1½ cups onion + 2 tsp garlic); cook until soft, 4–5 minutes. Add the Spices 1 (); cook 1 minute. Stir in the 2 Tbsp tomato paste and let it darken a shade, then deglaze with the ½ cup red wine, about 1 minute. Add the Tomato (1 can (14 oz) crushed tomatoes + 1 tsp sugar), then season with the 1 tsp salt and pepper. Simmer, partly covered, until thick, about 30 minutes; taste and adjust salt, keeping it light — the feta and cheese to come are salty. Meanwhile, boil the pasta and make the béchamel.

3
Boil the pasta and dress it
EquipmentA large pasta pot (6–8 qt) · a colander · a large mixing bowl

Bring the Salted water (4 qt water + 2 Tbsp salt) to a boil. Cook the 1 lb pasta 2 minutes short of the package time — it finishes in the oven — then drain. Toss the hot pasta with the Pasta dressing (3 egg whites + 3½ oz crumbled feta): the egg sets the bottom layer so it slices clean.

4
Make the béchamel
EquipmentA saucepan (3 qt) · a whisk

Warm the 4¼ cups milk. Melt the 7 Tbsp butter in the saucepan over medium heat, whisk in the 1 cup flour, and cook 2–3 minutes to lose the raw-flour taste. Add the warm milk a little at a time, whisking smooth between each, until it thickens to a heavy sauce. Off the heat, whisk in the Béchamel seasoning (¼ tsp nutmeg + ½ tsp salt + white pepper), most of the Cheese (1½ cups grated ; keep 2 Tbsp for the top), then the 3 egg yolks — off the heat, so they enrich without scrambling.

5
Bind and layer
EquipmentA deep baking dish (9×13 in) · the oven

Heat the oven to 350°F. Stir the 1 cup béchamel into the meat sauce — this binds the middle so the layers hold. Oil the dish; spread the pasta in an even bed, then the meat sauce, then pour the remaining béchamel over and smooth the top. Sprinkle with the reserved .

6
Bake
EquipmentThe oven · a timer

Bake at 350°F for about 45 minutes, until the top is golden with dark freckles and the centre is set when you jiggle the dish.

7
Rest, then serve
EquipmentA timer

Let it rest 20–30 minutes before cutting. This is not optional — it’s what lets the layers firm up so you lift clean squares instead of a landslide. Cut, lift, and serve.

A cook’s note

The rest at the end is the difference between a clean slice and a delicious mess — don’t skip it. Pastitsio is, if anything, better the next day; the layers set in the fridge and reheat beautifully, covered, in a low oven.
