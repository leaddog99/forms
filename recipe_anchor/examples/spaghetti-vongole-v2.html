Spaghettialle Vongole

👥 Serves 4⏱ 45 min (30 passive)🐚 Small vongole

Spaghetti alle vongole has nowhere to hide: a few ingredients, three minutes of real cooking, and the clams either give up a glossy, garlicky sauce or they don’t. This is the Tucci approach — no cream, no wine, no cheese — built to be cooked in one motion. Set your units and your chili form below, lay out your gear, and don’t light the burner until your guests are seated.

Method
1
Purge the clams
EquipmentA large mixing bowl (5 qt+) · a large colander

Soak 2 lb clams in cold water with 1 Tbsp cornmeal for 30 minutes; discard any that are cracked or won’t close when tapped. Drain and rinse. While they soak, set up everything below.

2
Salt the water; prep while it heats
EquipmentA large pot (6–8 qt) · a chef’s knife & board

Bring 4 qt water to a rolling boil with 3 Tbsp kosher salt (or 4 Tbsp Diamond Crystal) — about 1% salinity, and the pasta’s only seasoning, so go easy with salt after this. Meanwhile, sliver 2 Tbsp garlic, , and chop ¼ cup parsley, reserving 1 Tbsp to finish.

3
Cook the pasta two minutes short
EquipmentA timer · tongs or a spider · a heatproof measuring cup (1–2 cup)

When the water boils, add 1 lb spaghetti and pull it 2 minutes before the package’s al dente; it finishes in the pan. Before draining, reserve 1 cup of the starchy water.

4
Meanwhile, start the garlic-chili oil
EquipmentA heavy-bottomed sauté pan with lid (12 in) · a wooden spoon

Over low heat, warm ¼ cup olive oil with the garlic and for about 3 minutes, until the garlic is barely blond — never browned, which turns it bitter. Go in at the low end; you’ll fine-tune the heat at the finish.

5
Steam the clams open
EquipmentThe same sauté pan and lid (from step 4)

Raise the heat to medium, add the clams, pour in ¼ cup of the reserved water, cover, and steam 3–5 minutes until the shells gape. Discard any that stay shut.

6
Finish in the pan and serve
EquipmentWarm serving bowls (for 4)

Add the drained spaghetti and toss hard for 1 minute, loosening with another ¼–½ cup reserved water until the sauce turns glossy and coats every strand. Taste, then add more for heat or a pinch more salt only if it needs it. Off the heat, fold in 2 tsp raw olive oil, the reserved parsley, and ¼ tsp black pepper — no cheese. Divide among 4 warm bowls and serve at once.

A cook’s note

Vongole is at its peak the instant it leaves the pan, so seat your guests first and plate fast. Keep crusty bread on the table; the liquor pooled in the bottom of the bowl is the best bite, and bread is the only proper way to claim it.
