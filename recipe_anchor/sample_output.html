Method
1
Clean & soak the clams
EquipmentLarge mixing bowl (5 qt+) \u00B7 Large colander (5 qt+)

Clean the clams, discarding any with broken shells or any that won't close when you tap them. Soak the 2 lb clams in a bowl of cold water with 1 tbsp cornmeal for about half an hour, then drain in the colander and rinse to wash away grit and sand.

2
Boil the spaghetti
EquipmentLarge pot (6–8 qt) \u00B7 Tongs or a pasta fork

Bring the pot of water to a boil with a good amount of kosher salt, then cook the 1 lb spaghetti according to the package instructions. Time it so the pasta finishes right as the clams open.

3
Start the garlic & chili oil
EquipmentHeavy-bottomed sauté pan with lid (12 in) \u00B7 Wooden spoon

Meanwhile, in the sauté pan, heat ¼ cup extra-virgin olive oil over low heat. Add 3 cloves garlic, slivered and 2–4 dried peperoncini, and cook gently until the garlic is fragrant but not colored.

4
Add & season the clams
EquipmentHeavy-bottomed sauté pan with lid (12 in) (from step 3) \u00B7 Chef's knife & cutting board \u00B7 Tongs or a pasta fork (from step 2)

Raise the heat to medium and add the clams, shaking the pan and stirring to coat them in the oil and garlic. Season with a good amount of kosher salt and black pepper, then add 1 handful chopped parsley and toss to coat the clams once more.

5
Cover & steam open
EquipmentHeavy-bottomed sauté pan with lid (12 in) (from step 3) \u00B7 Timer

Cover the pan and cook, shaking it every so often, until the clams have opened and cooked through, about 3 minutes. Discard any that refuse to open.

6
Combine & serve
EquipmentLarge colander (5 qt+) (from step 1) \u00B7 Tongs or a pasta fork (from step 2) \u00B7 Serving bowls or a platter (for 4)

When the pasta and clams are both done, drain the spaghetti, add it to the pan with the clams, and toss it through. Garnish with a little extra chopped parsley and serve at once.
